# Fund

Fund is a personal decision-intelligence system: an external, searchable record of experiences, decisions, reasoning, lessons, and future policies.

Its purpose is not to make decisions on a person's behalf. It helps the person recover relevant context at the moment a decision is being made, including experiences they may no longer remember.

## Core loop

1. **Capture** a real event in the user's own words, with as little friction as possible.
2. **Clarify** what happened, what the user felt or believed, and what remains uncertain.
3. **Reflect** on decisions, reasoning, consequences, and lessons without treating interpretations as established facts.
4. **Commit** any chosen principle or future response as a policy, with its rationale and exceptions.
5. **Retrieve** relevant past experiences and policies when a new situation resembles them.
6. **Review** outcomes and revise policies when evidence changes.

## Non-negotiable design principles

- **Capture before interpretation.** Preserve the original account separately from AI-generated analysis.
- **Evidence and inference stay distinct.** Label facts, user interpretations, assistant hypotheses, and unresolved questions.
- **Recall must be useful at decision time.** Search should retrieve the reasoning and source event, not just a short lesson.
- **Policies are contextual, not absolute.** Store triggers, intended response, rationale, exceptions, and review conditions.
- **No silent rewriting of a person's values.** The user approves lessons and policies before they become active.
- **Contradictions are surfaced, not hidden.** New evidence may challenge an earlier lesson.
- **Privacy is foundational.** Personal event data must never be committed to this public repository.

## First release

- Fast event capture
- Structured event and decision records
- Lessons and user-approved policies
- Search and related-event retrieval
- A decision review flow
- Export and backup

AI-assisted analysis and semantic retrieval will be added only with clear provenance and user control.
